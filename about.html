About Irish Fishing Hub

Welcome to Irish Fishing Hub, the ultimate digital companion for anglers across Ireland and the United Kingdom. Whether you are casting a line into the deep Atlantic waters of the West Coast, targeting bass along the sandy shores of the South, or enjoying the tranquility of our inland freshwater lakes, our platform is designed to give you the exact data you need for a successful session.

Our Mission

Fishing is more than just a hobby—it's a tradition, a passion, and a way of life that connects us to nature and the ocean. Our mission is to modernize the angling experience across the British Isles and Ireland by providing real-time, highly accurate data in one centralized, easy-to-use application. For too long, anglers have had to rely on guessing the tides or switching between multiple conflicting weather apps to plan a simple fishing trip. We aim to change that by putting enterprise-grade meteorological and oceanographic data directly into the palm of your hand.

What We Provide

We aggregate crucial data points specific to the Irish coast and inland waterways to give you a comprehensive overview of the fishing conditions:

  • Live Tide Data & Predictions: Precise high and low tide times, tidal coefficients, and current water levels for hundreds of stations around the coast. We process this data dynamically so you never arrive at an empty beach again.
  • Weather & Marine Conditions: Wind speed, wind direction, atmospheric pressure, wave height, and localized forecasts that are critical for both your fishing success and your personal safety on the rocks.
  • Community Connections: A live, moderated social feed where anglers can share their recent catches, exchange bait tips, and discuss local hotspots securely without compromising their secret marks.
  • Tackle Shop & Slipway Directory: A comprehensive map of local tackle shops, boat slips, and marine facilities to ensure you are always stocked up and ready to launch.

Built by Anglers, For Anglers

Irish Fishing Hub was built from genuine frustration with clunky, outdated tide websites and inaccurate weather models. We wanted a premium, beautifully designed interface that works flawlessly on mobile devices while out on the rocks, standing on the pier, or sitting on a boat. The ocean is a harsh environment, and your tools need to be reliable, fast, and easy to read in direct sunlight.

We are continually updating our features based on direct feedback from the vibrant Irish fishing community. Every update, from the Solunar Peak Feeding radar to the depth charts, is built to solve a real problem faced by local anglers.

The Community

Our Commitment to Sustainability

We strongly advocate for sustainable fishing practices, adherence to local catch limits, and the catch-and-release of vulnerable species such as European Sea Bass and all sharks/rays. The future of our sport depends on the health of our oceans, and we aim to educate and encourage our users to respect the marine environment.
