SECTION 1: OVERVIEW & TOURNAMENT MECHANICS

The Irish Fishing Hub All-Ireland Species Hunt is a 100% free, year-long angling tournament designed to aggressively drive user acquisition, boost website traffic, and saturate Irish social media feeds with user-generated content.

Core Concepts

Low-Barrier Entry: Anglers enter for free by catching a fish and tagging Irish Fishing Hub on social media or uploading directly to the website.

The Ultimate Equalizer: Scoring is based on biological variety, not size or weight. A 3-inch Three-Spined Stickleback or Corkwing Wrasse scores exactly the same (1 point) as a 20lb Pike or a Specimen Tope. This levels the playing field between kids, casual shore anglers, and deep-sea boaters.

Data Centralization: Social media posts act as marketing funnels, driving traffic back to irishfishinghub.com where the official master leaderboard resides.

Step-by-Step Multi-Platform Submission Workflow

Account Creation: The angler visits irishfishinghub.com to create a free account and download the printable Official IFH Token.

The Catch: The angler catches a fish using fair rod-and-line methods.

The Proof: The angler takes a clear photo or video featuring the fish, the angler, and the physical token (or a clear image of the token on a second phone screen).

The Submission: Within 48 hours, the angler uploads the image. They can either post it directly onto the Irish Fishing Hub community web feed or publish it on Instagram, Facebook, or TikTok.

The Tagging Engine: If using social media, the user must tag @IrishFishingHub and include the mandatory competition hashtag: #IFHSpeciesHunt26.

📋 Official Tournament Rules

1. Scoring & Species Limits
Every distinct, legally caught species awards 1 point.
Size does not matter! A minnow, stickleback, or blenny counts exactly the same as a trophy pike, salmon, or blue shark.
To keep the leaderboard accurate, a maximum of one entry per species will count toward your final score. You can upgrade your catch photo later if you catch a larger specimen, but your point total for that species remains at 1.

2. Fair Angling & Catch Methods
All fish must be caught legally using a rod, reel, line, and hook.
Strictly no netting, trapping, or foul-hooking.
Lines must not be left unattended. All fish must be hooked, fought, and landed by the entering angler.

3. Strict Conservation & Legal Compliance
This is a 100% Catch & Release tournament. All fish must be photographed immediately and returned safely to the water before you resume fishing.
All entries must strictly adhere to Inland Fisheries Ireland (IFI) laws, local bye-laws, and EU maritime regulations.
Highly regulated species (such as Atlantic Salmon, Sea Trout, and Sea Bass) must be handled with extreme care. Targeting protected species during closed seasons, failing to follow strict catch-and-release rules for bass, or fishing for salmonids without the appropriate state licenses will result in immediate disqualification.

4. Verification & Disqualification
The Irish Fishing Hub judging panel reserves the right to reject any photo where the fish cannot be positively identified, or if the official token is missing or illegible.
Any evidence of photoshopping, recycling old photos, or mistreatment of fish will result in an immediate lifetime ban from all Irish Fishing Hub events.
